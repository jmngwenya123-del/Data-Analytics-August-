Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project are to:
Identify the products that generate the highest revenue.
Determine which stores perform best during the morning period, from 06:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
Analyse sales trends across different time intervals.
Compare revenue performance across products, stores, and trading periods.
Identify sales patterns and opportunities for business growth.
Provide recommendations to improve sales performance and increase revenue.
Develop visual dashboards to communicate key findings effectively.

Recommendations for Improving Sales Performance
Based on the project's objectives and identified sales patterns, the following recommendations are proposed.
1 Prioritise Coffee Sales

Maintain sufficient coffee stock, especially during peak morning hours.
Identify the most popular coffee products and monitor their individual sales performance.
Introduce coffee promotions and product bundles where profitable.
Monitor coffee sales regularly to avoid stockouts and lost sales.

2 Maximise Morning Sales
Ensure that sufficient employees are available between 06:00 AM and 12:00 PM.
Prepare equipment, ingredients, and other essential supplies before the morning rush.
Improve order preparation and service efficiency to reduce customer waiting times.
Consider breakfast and coffee combinations to increase average transaction values

3 Improve Sales During Quieter Periods
Introduce targeted promotions during slower periods.
Test afternoon coffee-and-snack offers to encourage additional purchases.

4 Improve Store Performance
Compare revenue across individual store locations.
Identify successful practices at the best-performing stores.

5 Use Data for Inventory Planning
Align inventory replenishment with historical sales patterns.
Prioritise the availability of high-revenue products.
Monitor stock levels to reduce stockouts and unnecessary excess inventory.
Use regular sales reports to support purchasing and replenishment decisions.

6 Monitor Sales Performance Regularly
Review revenue and product performance on a daily and weekly basis.
